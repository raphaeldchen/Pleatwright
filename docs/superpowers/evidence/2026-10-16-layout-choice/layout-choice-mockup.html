The layout choice, in the app

A sketch using real data: dolphin_0, which packs at grid 50 with mirror symmetry or at grid 32 without it. Both versions solve, certify and fold. The chat panel is on the left and the viewer on the right, as in the app today.

Tree approved · Packing
Your dolphin can be packed two ways. Which do you want?
Smaller grid · grid 32 (Recommended)
  • Pro: the folded dolphin is 1.56× larger from the same sheet
  • Pro: about half the creases (129 vertices against 257), with fewer hidden layers per flap
  • Con: the crease pattern isn't mirror-symmetric, so the left and right sides fold differently
Mirror-symmetric · grid 50
  • Pro: the left and right halves of the sheet mirror each other, so each paired step is folded twice the same way
  • Con: the model comes out 36% smaller
  • Con: the band of extra paper (top of the pattern) is folded into the flaps, so they come out thicker
The question only appears when the two layouts differ by a meaningful amount; otherwise the symmetric layout is used automatically.
