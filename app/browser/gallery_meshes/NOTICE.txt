The gallery's meshes, one per subject.

Each was generated from a photograph by tencent/Hunyuan3D-2
(https://huggingface.co/spaces/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2; /shape_generation, steps 30,
guidance 5.0, seed 1234, octree resolution 256, background removal on), then
lightened for the web: connected pieces under 50 faces dropped and the rest
decimated to about 100,000 faces by quadric edge collapse. The source
photographs are not included.
